# Bitcoin Wizard / Magic Internet Money

**Source**: Know Your Meme entry at `/memes/magic-internet-money-bitcoin-wizard`
**Image**: `https://i.kym-cdn.com/entries/icons/facebook/000/036/711/magic.jpg`

## Origins

- **February 19, 2013** — u/theymos (r/Bitcoin admin) asked for ad submissions to promote the subreddit.
- Same day, Redditor **u/mavensbot** submitted an MS Paint illustration of a wizard captioned **"Bitcoin Magic Internet Money"** (deliberately crude/trollish).
- **November 6, 2013** — posted to r/ads by an unknown user; hit **1,600+ points (98% upvoted)**, 585 comments.
- Reddit's ad team polished the design into a proper site-wide ad.

## Price Impact

| Date | Event | BTC Price |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Nov 6, 2013 | Ad goes live | $287 |
| Nov 13, 2013 | South Park episode airs | $416 |
| Nov 29, 2013 | Peak rally | **$1,132** |

r/Bitcoin subscribership grew 36% to 74k subs. Many credited the ad with helping drive the 2013 bull run.

## South Park Connection

**Season 17, Episode 8** — *"The World According to Snacks"* (aired Nov 13, 2013) featured a Bitcoin segment with a wizard illustration strikingly similar to the Reddit ad. Believed to be a deliberate homage.

## Legacy

- "Magic Internet Money" became a perennial Bitcoin meme (used affectionately and mockingly).
- Sister memes: **"This Is Good For Bitcoin"** (sarcastic cope), **#LaserRayUntil100K** (laser-eyed profile pics).
- Still referenced as a symbol of Bitcoin's chaotic grassroots early days.
